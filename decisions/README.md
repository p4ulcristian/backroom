# backroom decisions

One file per decision: `YYYY-MM-DD-what-we-decided.md`. The title is the decision. Each file says what we decided, why, and what we chose against. A decision changes by a new file; the old one says so at the top (`Replaced by` or `Changed in part by`), and nothing else in it is rewritten.

The first two were made while backroom was a library inside Groundwork, a Clojure framework (groundwork.server.background-jobs, and the jobs of an older Groundwork before that); they name its parts (groundwork.sql, groundwork.schema) where they came from. The ones they build on stayed with Groundwork: "Groundwork's own tables are table maps, in their own folder", and the naming of the work: "The jobs library is named groundwork.background-jobs", "The background work is called chores", "The background work is called background jobs". Groundwork now names both the family of libraries this one belongs to and the composer that puts them together; the decisions that stayed with Groundwork are in the composer's `decisions/`.
